Kart Watch GP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Kart Watch GP is an Apple Watch game made by Julio Aris Serey. This policy explains what the game stores, including the optional Online mode (Versus and Worldwide).

Playing offline

The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your progress (unlocked cups, medals and best times) is stored on your watch and in the iCloud/watch backups managed by Apple.

Online mode (optional)

Online mode is only used if you open it. It stores the following data, linked to a per-device identifier derived from your iCloud account, and used only to run the feature (app functionality):

  • Generated nickname and avatar. The game assigns them; there is no free text, so you cannot type a name or message.
  • Game statistics: wins, best result and the resulting ranking score.
  • Country or region, taken from your device settings, shown as a flag in the Worldwide ranking.
  • Challenges: who challenged whom, the track seed and the scores.
  • Reports and blocks you send about other players, to keep the community safe.

This data is kept in Apple's CloudKit public database for this app. We do not run our own servers. We do not use this data for tracking, advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Game Center. Live matches and the ranking use Apple Game Center, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Live matches are played through your iPhone, which sends only race positions (position, speed, lap) to the other players in the match. There is no chat and no user-generated content.

Nearby races. "Same Wi-Fi" races use your local network (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth). Only race positions are exchanged directly between the watches; nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full Game" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and contains no chat or user-generated content. Online mode follows Apple's Screen Time and Game Center restrictions: multiplayer features are disabled when a parent restricts them.

Retention and deletion

Online data is kept while your profile exists. You can delete it at any time in the game: Options → Delete my online data removes your profile, your challenges and local caches. Reports about other players may be kept for safety reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or the LGPD in Brazil) you can ask what data we hold about you, request a copy, correct it or have it deleted, and object to its processing. Most of this you can do yourself with the delete option above; for anything else write to us and we will reply within 30 days.
