Support & help

Pair your glasses

On the Apple Watch open SportsRings › “Glasses”, choose the code shown on your glasses (FIT-XXXX) and confirm. You only need to do this once.

Install on Rokid Glasses

Download the app from ariagames.dev/sportsrings and open it with the Rokid app on your iPhone.

Heart rate or steps look wrong?

If you carry several devices, set Apple Watch first in Health › Steps › Data Sources & Access. SportsRings reads the combined totals from Apple Health.

3D map is empty

The map needs internet (or an offline zone downloaded earlier) and Location access while using the app.
