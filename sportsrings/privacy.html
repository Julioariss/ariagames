SportsRings

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026. ARIAgames (“we”) makes SportsRings for iPhone, Apple Watch and Rokid Glasses.

Short version

We do not collect, transmit to our servers, sell or share your health, workout, location or identity data, and we keep it in no database of ours. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your device

To work, the apps keep a few things only on your own devices: (1) the latest workout value shown by the watch complication, cleared when the workout ends; (2) downloaded map tiles and the offline map zones you chose (they can reveal places you visited or chose); (3) the pairing code of your paired glasses. On the Rokid Glasses app (Android), health values exist only in memory while the app is open; it stores the pairing code and, if you request them, offline map zones; backups are disabled. You can erase this from SportsRings › Glasses › Privacy › “Delete data stored on this iPhone”, or by uninstalling the apps.

Apple Health

With your permission SportsRings reads activity, heart rate and workout data from Apple Health to show your rings, and saves the workouts you start in the app back to Apple Health. This data stays on your devices under your control. Heart rate and metrics travel by Bluetooth from your Apple Watch to your glasses and are shown live; the glasses keep them only in memory while the app is open and do not store them. You can revoke access anytime in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Location

Your Apple Watch/iPhone location is used during a workout (or while the 3D map is open) to show your position and route. Location is not sent to us. Routes are saved only to Apple Health, if you start a workout.

Maps and the internet

To draw maps, the app downloads map tiles from third-party providers (OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap data, AWS terrain data and Apple Maps). These requests contain approximate tile coordinates (an area of about 2 km) and your IP address, never health or workout data. Those providers have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Not a medical device

SportsRings does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. The pulse line is a visualization of heart rate, not an electrocardiogram. Consult a health professional about symptoms.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To delete workouts, use the Health app.
