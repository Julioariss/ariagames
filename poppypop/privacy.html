Poppy Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Poppy Pop (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch) is made by Julio Aris Serey ("we"). We do not sell data, show ads, use analytics or include third-party SDKs, and we do not track you.

The game offers optional in-app purchases (coins, lives and the full game). Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Offline play

If you only play the story and endless modes, your progress and high scores stay on your device and in your Apple iCloud/device backups. We receive nothing.

Online modes (Versus and Worldwide)

These optional modes use Apple services (Game Center and iCloud/CloudKit). When you use them, a gameplay profile is stored in our CloudKit public database, linked to a random identifier and not to your name, email or Apple ID:

  • an automatically generated nickname and an avatar you pick from the game (you cannot type free text);
  • gameplay stats: wins, best chain, "voltage" score and Worldwide challenge scores;
  • your country/region, taken from your device's regional settings (used only to show a flag; we do not read your location);
  • challenges you send or answer, and reports you file about other players.

Other players can see your nickname, avatar, flag and stats in the rankings and when you play together. Friends and matchmaking are provided by Apple Game Center under Apple's privacy policy. "Nearby duel" uses Apple's local network (MultipeerConnectivity) to find a device next to you; it does not send data to us.

There is no chat, voice or free-text messaging between players. You can report a player in the app; reports are reviewed and abusive profiles are removed.

Children

The game is suitable for children. We do not collect names, emails, photos, contacts or precise location, and players cannot exchange messages. Parents can disable Game Center multiplayer with Screen Time restrictions on the device.

Retention and your rights

Online profile data is kept while your profile is active. You can delete your online profile and all associated records at any time from the Versus screen in the app (delete my data), or by writing to us. Under GDPR/CCPA/LGPD and similar laws you may request access, correction or deletion; we will answer within 30 days.
