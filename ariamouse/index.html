Setup

Ready in 3 steps.

Install on your MacDownload ARIAmouse for Mac below, open the disk image and drag ARIAmouse to Applications. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open. Allow Bluetooth and, when asked, let it control your Mac (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility).
Install the Apple Watch appARIAmouse for Apple Watch on the App Store.
Use itOpen ARIAmouse on your Watch. It connects to your Mac by itself. Slide, tap and scroll.
