ARIAmouse

Your Apple Watch becomes a trackpad for Android. Tap to click, slide to move the cursor, swipe the edges to go back or forward. Works with Android phones and smart glasses.

Privacy. ARIAmouse collects no data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Gestures from the Watch go directly to your Android device over Bluetooth and are never stored or sent anywhere else. Bluetooth permissions are used only to connect your Watch. Notifications are used only for the "connected" notice while the service runs.
Accessibility service (Android). The optional accessibility service is used only to perform the pointer, click, scroll and back/forward actions you make on the Watch, and to relaunch ARIAmouse from a glasses gesture. It does not read, record or transmit screen content or anything you type. You can turn it off any time in Android Settings.
