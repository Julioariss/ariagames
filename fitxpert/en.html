Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. CONTROLLER

The controller is Julio Aris Serey (ARIAgames), Santiago, Chile. Privacy contact: privacidad@ariagames.dev.

2. CORE PRINCIPLE: YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE

FITxpert works without accounts, without our own servers, without ads, analytics or tracking. We do not receive, see or store your training or health data. Everything is stored encrypted on your device.

3. DATA THE APP PROCESSES (ON YOUR DEVICE)

  • Basic identification: the name or nickname you enter.
  • Sports profile: sport, level, goal, training days and mode (athlete/coach).
  • Health and wellness data (SENSITIVE / SPECIAL CATEGORY / CONSUMER HEALTH DATA): body weight, sessions, loads, reps, perceived exertion (RPE/sRPE), sleep, energy, muscle soreness and stress.
  • Apple Health data (READ-ONLY and only if you authorize it): heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, respiration, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, VO₂max, heart rate recovery, walking asymmetry, active energy, exercise minutes and weight. Processed on your iPhone to compute your body status; NEVER sent to iCloud, servers or your coach, never used for advertising, and nothing is written to Apple Health.
  • Physical-activity readiness result: only whether we recommend seeing a doctor; your individual answers are not stored.
  • Cryptographic keys generated on your iPhone to encrypt data and link with your coach.

4. PURPOSES

  • a) Generate and adapt your training plan; b) compute indicators (readiness, load, estimated 1RM, ranks); c) if you choose, share with your coach the categories you authorize. Never used for advertising, commercial profiling, sale, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Automatic recommendations are guidance only.

5. LEGAL BASIS

Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a); Chile Law 19.628 as amended by Law 21.719, Art. 16; LGPD Art. 11(I); and equivalent laws). You can withdraw it at any time, without giving reasons, in Plan › Legal & privacy.

6. COACH LINKS AND ICLOUD

Data from Apple Health is excluded from any transmission. Only if you link with a coach, the app uses iCloud (CloudKit, Apple Inc.) as a transport channel. Everything is end-to-end encrypted (X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305): neither Apple nor we can read it. Exchange records are deleted once the link is completed. Your coach is an independent third party and controller of what you choose to share; see "Coach Terms". Sharing with a coach requires a separate consent you give when approving each link.

7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

iCloud infrastructure may be located outside your country. Only encrypted information that cannot identify you without your keys transits it; Apple acts as an infrastructure provider without access to content.

8. RETENTION

Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Invite codes expire after 15 minutes. We keep no copies.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, blocking and withdrawal of consent. Exercise them in-app (Plan › Legal & privacy: "Export my data", "Withdraw consent", "Delete all my data") or by writing to privacidad@ariagames.dev. We respond within the legal deadlines. You may complain to your data protection authority (e.g. Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency, your EU/UK supervisory authority, Brazil's ANPD). We do not sell or share personal data (CCPA/CPRA) and do not engage in targeted advertising.

10. SECURITY

Encryption at rest with a key in the iPhone Keychain (this device only), complete file protection, Face ID/passcode lock, end-to-end encryption for links, no user search and safety-number verification. If a breach affecting your data occurred, we would notify the authorities and affected users as required by law.

11. CHILDREN

Intended for users 18+. Users aged 14–17 (or under the digital age of consent in their country) may use it only with parental or guardian permission. Not permitted for children under 14. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (COPPA).

12. CHANGES

We will show changes in the app and ask for your consent again when required by law.

13. CONTACT

Health Notice

FITxpert does NOT provide medical advice.

  • See a doctor before starting any exercise program, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes or another chronic disease, are pregnant or postpartum, take medication, have an injury, or have not trained for a long time.
  • Stop immediately and seek medical care if you feel chest pain or pressure, dizziness, fainting, palpitations, disproportionate shortness of breath or sharp pain. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (e.g. 911, 112, 999, 131 in Chile).
  • Plyometrics, sprints and maximal loads require correct technique and, for beginners, professional supervision.
  • The "injury risk" indicator (ACWR) is a statistical load metric, not a medical assessment.
